3Common problems faced

Manpower, inward

  • A team lead did not perform up to their role.
  • Lack of initiative among members.
  • Lack of experience from engineers and team leads; the Tech Director had to step in.
  • Balancing productivity against personal growth.
  • Overly vocal members who talk more than they contribute.
  • Many members check out as the semester goes on.

Manpower, outward

  • Not all parties are well-versed in tech.
  • Expect many requests that may have simpler solutions. Don't just say yes.
  • Timelining is important.

4Learnings from last AY's recruitment

  • Interviews focused on interest and overlooked availability and commitment.
  • Group interviews help show how engineers interact with each other.
  • Less experienced applicants tend to be more committed. Do not discount inexperienced people.
  • The technically stronger candidates may not be as available.

5Proposed new structure

Guiding considerations

  • Most members will be inexperienced; balance output against educational value.
  • Agile velocity may be hard for newer members to adapt to, so gauge pace by personal availability.
  • Each iteration needs time for development, learning new tech, and balancing studies.
  • Plan around stakeholders' timelines.
  • Lean more heavily on tech leads as a fallback, and set up a good educational environment for engineers.
  • Check headcount against the plan with HGS.

Team notes

Team A

What is the handoff process from A to Teams B, C and D? If there is a "one true way" to do things, other teams may feel less ownership.

Team B

Outreach channels: posters, word of mouth, house chats, Battle Royale pubs. Consider pull vs. push.

Team C

Previous wikis leaned towards student life; the new one targets Danelle's vision and SOP documentation, which is currently heavily scattered.

Collating, auditing and posting knowledge requires heavy team involvement.

Team D

More ad-hoc work. ROI may be low since projects are one-off, and making things generalisable could restrict NOW's vision.

Roles & sizing

  • Team Lead and PM could be the same person.
  • Expected ratio of people who direct to people who build is roughly 2 : 4.
  • Does it make sense to dedicate 2 people solely to UI/UX, or should it be a shared decision across teams? People rarely move across teams, which undercuts the benefit of cross-team consistency.
  • Open tension between uniformity and autonomy.
  • Consider a dedicated pubs team or individual.

6Operations & process

Meetings

  • Bi-weekly plus monthly meetings is a high commitment, and finding common times is hard.
  • Don't underestimate how hard it is to get everyone into one meeting.
  • Hold leadership meetings more often than full-team meetings: there's less value in everyone attending.

Sprints

  • Iterate as you go. If 2-week sprints are too short to get much done, stretch the cycle.

Tooling & documentation

  • There was a lack of documentation previously.
  • Use shared credentials rather than individually managed accounts.
  • Set up a feedback channel.
  • Set up logging and metrics.

Onboarding & adoption

  • What stakeholders promise may not match real-world implementation and adoption.

7Culture & stakeholder relationship

  • The Tech Directorate's relationship with the rest of the MC/committee should be symbiotic, not client and consultant.
  • Projects are heavily aligned with the Exco's manifestos, so they do have a stake in the projects succeeding.
  • Tech is less prominent to residents than events or food.
  • Residents are unlikely to visit the website; everything goes through Battle Royale.
  • Distinguish between improving processes and building something that automates away all the processes and human interaction.

8Architecture notes

  • The previous Tech Director tried to move towards micro-services.
  • nusc.club and LMS currently each have their own sign-in.
  • The intent was for nusc.club to be the source of truth for logins and user info. This may already be implemented on nusc.club, but not yet on LMS.
  • nusc.club has an event booking feature. Open question: does it carry weight with VP CL, or is it purely aesthetic?
    • Noted: the website is the single source of truth. All events must go through the website.
